OUR STORY

Built for the creators who need more than a timeline editor.

Mercy Videos was founded with a simple mission: to provide world-class video editing services that are fast, affordable, and high-quality. We recognized that creators and businesses were struggling to find reliable video editors who truly understood their vision and could deliver consistently.

What started as a small team has grown into a trusted partner for 50+ clients across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and beyond. Every project we take on reflects our commitment to excellence, attention to detail, and passion for visual storytelling.

Today, we're proud to be one of the most trusted video editing agencies for YouTubers, podcasters, real estate professionals, and businesses of all sizes.
