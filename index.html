Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical turnaround time?

expand_more

Standard YouTube videos are typically delivered within 1-2days hours. Short-form content like Reels, Shorts, and TikToks is usually completed within 48h-72h days. Larger projects such as documentaries, commercials, or corporate videos generally take 1–2 weeks, depending on complexity and scope.

Do you offer unlimited revisions?

expand_more

Yes, we want you to be 100% satisfied. We offer unlimited reasonable revisions on all our projects to ensure the final product matches your exact vision.

How do we share files?

expand_more

We typically use Frame.io, Google Drive, or Dropbox for fast and secure file transfers. Once you sign up, we'll set up a dedicated shared folder for your raw footage and final exports.

Can you manage my YouTube channel end to end?

expand_more

Yes. Our YouTube management service can support content planning, editing, thumbnails, upload preparation, titles, descriptions, and optimization so your channel stays consistent.

What do you need to start a project?

expand_more

We usually need your raw footage, brand guidelines if available, reference videos, editing notes, and the platform or format you want the final video delivered for.
